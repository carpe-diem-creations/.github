Carpe Diem Creations is an independent mobile app studio based in Dover, Delaware, that designs, builds, and ships every app in its portfolio in house. Rather than chasing a single category, the studio builds a shelf of focused, single purpose apps for everyday problems, from connecting an Android watch to an iPhone, to identifying birds by song, to keeping track of medication schedules. The team uses its own apps daily, which shapes a product philosophy built on simplicity and dependability over feature bloat. Carpe Diem Creations also takes on app development work for outside clients, applying the same in house design and build process used across its own twelve app portfolio.

## ⌚ Lynio

Lynio bridges the gap between Android smartwatches and iPhone, something Samsung and Google do not officially support. Once paired, calls, notifications, contacts, and health data from a Galaxy Watch or Wear OS device sync straight to iPhone, no Android phone required after setup. It works with Galaxy Watch, Google Pixel Watch, Xiaomi, OnePlus, Fossil, TicWatch, and more, with new devices added regularly. Setup takes about three minutes, and thousands of watch owners who switched to iPhone use it daily to keep their watch fully functional.

## 🎙️ Oryn

Oryn is an AI powered audio to text transcription tool built for meetings, lectures, and interviews. Speaker diarization identifies who said what, so a transcript reads like a real conversation instead of a wall of text. Oryn then generates an intelligent summary with actionable items that can be added to other apps such as a calendar, or exported and shared with a team. It handles unlimited transcription length, generates cross language summaries, and offers Fast or Deep Accuracy modes depending on the job, with noise isolation to keep speech clear in busy environments.

## 📺 TVGO

TVGO turns a smartphone into a universal remote for Android TV, Google TV, and 50 plus other smart TV brands, including Hisense, Samsung, LG, Sony, TCL, and Philips. Weighly estimates the weight of almost anything from a single photo using AI, no scale required. It is available in ten languages and built for anyone curious, shipping something, or just without a scale handy.
