# Carpe Diem Creations

Independent mobile app studio based in Dover, Delaware, designing, building, and shipping every app in its portfolio in house. Rather than chasing a single category, we build a shelf of focused, single-purpose apps for everyday problems — from connecting an Android watch to an iPhone, to identifying birds by song, to keeping track of medication schedules.

🌐 [carpediemcreations.io](https://carpediemcreations.io) · ✉️ [hello@carpediemcreations.io](mailto:hello@carpediemcreations.io)

## Apps

### ⌚ [Lynio](https://lynio.carpediemcreations.io) — Android Watch to iPhone Connectivity

Bridges the gap between Android smartwatches and iPhone. Once paired, calls, notifications, contacts, and health data from a Galaxy Watch or Wear OS device sync straight to iPhone, no Android phone required after setup.
[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.carpediemcreations.lynio) · [Website](https://lynio.carpediemcreations.io)

### 💬 [Kindu Sexy](https://kindusexy.com) — Intimacy for Couples

An intimacy app for couples, with 200+ curated positions, couple games like Truth or Dare, a foreplay wheel, and spicy trivia. Progress tracking lets couples earn points and unlock badges together. For consenting adults 18+.
[Website](https://kindusexy.com)

### 🎙️ [Oryn](https://oryn.carpediemcreations.io) — AI Audio Transcription

AI powered audio to text transcription for meetings, lectures, and interviews. Speaker diarization identifies who said what, and Oryn generates intelligent summaries with actionable items ready to export or share.
[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6756976491) · [Website](https://oryn.carpediemcreations.io)

### 🐾 [Furry Friend](https://furryfriend.app) — Pet Care

[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6472447402) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tirgei.furryfriend) · [Website](https://furryfriend.app)

### 🍸 [Pocket Bartender](https://pocketbartender.app) — Cocktail Recipes

### 📺 [TVGO](https://tvgo.carpediemcreations.io) — Universal TV Remote

Turns a smartphone into a universal remote for Android TV, Google TV, and 50+ other smart TV brands, including Hisense, Samsung, LG, Sony, TCL, and Philips. Keyboard search, quick app launch, full media controls, and a built-in movies and TV shows guide help users discover what's on, get notified when a title airs, and rate what they've watched. Family Sharing lets a household set up once and control TVs from their own phones, with multiple TVs manageable from the same app.
[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6756779477) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.carpediemcreations.tvremote) · [Website](https://tvgo.carpediemcreations.io)

### ⚖️ [Weighly](https://weighly.carpediemcreations.io) — AI Weight Estimator

Estimates the weight of almost anything from a single photo using AI, no scale required, with a Guess the Grams mode that turns estimating into a game. Available in ten languages.
[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.carpediemcreations.weightscales) · [Website](https://weighly.carpediemcreations.io)
